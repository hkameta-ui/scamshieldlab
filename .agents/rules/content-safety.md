# Rule: Content Ethics & Non-Offensive Standards

1. **Google AdSense & SafeSearch Compliance**:
   - The platform strictly prohibits offensive language, slurs, profanity, vulgarity, hate speech, sexual content, harassment, or violent imagery.
   - All content must qualify for Google AdSense Family-Safe classification to ensure eligibility for top-tier advertising rates.
2. **Victim Empathy & Support**:
   - Provide respectful, dignified, and constructive guidance for individuals experiencing fraud.
   - Maintain an objective, educational cybersecurity tone without sensationalism.
3. **FTC Transparency**:
   - Commercial affiliate relationships must be transparently and conspicuously disclosed as mandated by the Federal Trade Commission.
